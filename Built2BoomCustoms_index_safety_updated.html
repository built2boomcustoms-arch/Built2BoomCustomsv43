Built With Safety in Mind

Every Built2BoomCustoms speaker is designed and assembled with battery care and electrical safety in mind. Our builds include low-voltage battery protection and multiple fuses to help protect your power system and equipment during normal use.

Automatic Battery Protection

Our battery protection devices monitor battery voltage and automatically shut off power to the speaker when the voltage drops to a preset level. This low-voltage cutoff helps prevent excessive discharge, which can shorten battery life or cause permanent battery damage. The cutoff is intended to protect your battery, but it does not replace proper charging, storage, and battery care.

Multiple Safety Fuses

We install multiple fuses in our speaker systems to help protect electrical circuits. A fuse is a protective component designed to interrupt current if it rises above a safe level, such as during an overload or short circuit. This can help limit damage to wiring and electronic components and reduce the risk of overheating or fire.

Safety features reduce certain risks but cannot eliminate them. Always use compatible batteries and chargers, keep equipment dry, avoid damaged batteries, and never bypass or replace fuses with incorrect ratings. For more information, read our Safety & Liability Policy.
