Payments are handled through the checkout provider used by the website. Payment, hosting, and shipping providers may process information needed to deliver their services under their own privacy policies. Do not email us your complete card number or security code.

Order information is used to fulfill purchases, communicate about production and shipping, answer support requests, and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations.
