Safety & Liability

Please read these important instructions before operating your custom portable speaker system. Proper use, charging, storage, and maintenance help reduce the risk of electrical damage, overheating, fire, or personal injury.

Safe operation and battery use

  • Use only compatible batteries, chargers, power adapters, and accessories appropriate for your specific speaker configuration.
  • Follow the battery and charger manufacturer's instructions. Never use a damaged, swollen, leaking, overheating, or otherwise unsafe battery.
  • Do not alter wiring, bypass safety features, modify the power system, or attempt unauthorized electrical repairs.
  • Keep the speaker away from flammable materials, extreme heat, and excessive moisture. Do not submerge the speaker or expose it to heavy rain.
  • Do not leave charging batteries unattended or charge them near combustible materials. Disconnect power and stop using the product immediately if you notice unusual heat, smoke, odor, sparks, or damage.
  • Keep the system in a well-ventilated area and away from children when charging or servicing.

Water exposure

Current speaker models are water-resistant, not waterproof. Avoid submersion, heavy rain, and excessive moisture. A fully waterproof and floatable model is in development and is not yet available.
