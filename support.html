Customer Support

Need help with a build, an order, payment, or a custom request? Start here.

Before Ordering

Can I change my build?

Yes, while you are still in the configurator. Review your selections carefully before paying because custom orders may enter production after payment.

Does the displayed price include everything I selected?

Yes. The final build summary is intended to show the selected options and the full purchase price before checkout.

Do I have to pay a deposit?

No. Built2BoomCustoms is set up for full payment at checkout.

After Ordering

I received the wrong item or my order arrived damaged.

Contact us as soon as possible with your order information and photos of the issue so we can review it.

I need to cancel or change my order.

Contact us as soon as possible. Requests may be considered if production has not started, but changes are not guaranteed once production begins.

Contact Support

For order-specific help, include your name, order number if available, and a short description of what you need help with. You can also include photos when they are relevant.
