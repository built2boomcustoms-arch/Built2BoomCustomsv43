Return Policy

Because Built2BoomCustoms builds each speaker box to the customer's selected configuration, custom orders are handled differently from standard retail products.

Custom Orders

All custom-built speaker boxes are made specifically from the options selected and paid for by the customer. Because of this, custom orders are final sale once production has begun, except where required by applicable law.

Before You Pay

Please review your build carefully before completing payment. Your checkout page will show the selected box, color, speakers, subwoofer, power system, extras, amplifier, and full purchase price.

  • Make sure your box size and color are correct.
  • Verify speaker and subwoofer quantities and sizes.
  • Check your power-system selection and custom wording/logo details.
  • Confirm your contact information before paying.

Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If your order arrives damaged, or if you receive an item that does not match the configuration you purchased, contact customer support as soon as possible with your order information and clear photos. We will review the issue and work with you on an appropriate resolution.

Cancellations

If you need to cancel or change an order, contact us as soon as possible. Requests made before production begins may be considered, but changes cannot be guaranteed after an order has entered production.

Questions?

If you're unsure about a return, cancellation, damage claim, or order issue, please contact us through the customer support page.
