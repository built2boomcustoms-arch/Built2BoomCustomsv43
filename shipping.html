Shipping & Production

Every Built2BoomCustoms speaker system is made to your selected configuration.

Production time

Estimated production time is 7–14 business days after an order is accepted. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed completion date. Production times may change based on parts availability, custom finishes, and order volume.

Shipping rates

Shipping is calculated at checkout based on your delivery address and available shipping options. Please review the full shipping charge and final total before paying.

If your checkout does not show a shipping charge or a clear shipping option before payment, please contact us before ordering. Shipping calculation must be supported by the payment checkout system.

Delivery

Transit time begins after production is completed and the order is handed to the carrier. Transit estimates and tracking, when available, depend on the carrier and shipping service selected.

Shipping questions

Email built2boomcustoms@gmail.com with your order details. Please do not send payment-card details by email.
